# Curated YouTube opening passages

Public community transcripts of educational YouTube channels. This broadens beyond the original sleep-history channel set, whose caption fetcher failed. Wording checked against cached archive text, not independently against audio.

Exact contiguous opening passages. Greetings, sponsors and preamble may precede the selected passage and are explicitly retained. Complete hooks can require several sentences; these are not claimed to be 50 great literal first sentences.
