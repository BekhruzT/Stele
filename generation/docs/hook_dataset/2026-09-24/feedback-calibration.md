The correction is to make an ordinary expectation and a concrete departure from
it audible. A clear explanation, a scientist being surprised, or an unexplained
impressive quantity does not supply that departure. Stop when the turn lands;
letters, exile and apparatus belong in subsequent prose. An analogy may make a
scientific result accessible, but must preserve the relationship actually tested.
Do not invent an economic rule such as one person cannot move a market.
